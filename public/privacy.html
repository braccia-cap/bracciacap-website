Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

1. Who we are

BracciaCap AI is a software platform operated by Braccia Capital Inc., a Wyoming corporation ("we," "us," "our"). We provide autonomous AI agents that assist Registered Investment Advisors ("RIAs") with operational tasks. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website and platform.

2. Information we collect

  • Information you provide: name, work email, firm name, phone number, and details you submit through our demo-request form or during onboarding.
  • Client and account data: when an RIA connects custodial or CRM systems, we process data about that firm's accounts and clients solely to perform the services the firm directs.
  • Usage data: log data, device and browser information, and analytics about how the platform is used.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the platform and its AI agents.
  • To respond to inquiries, schedule demos, and provide support.
  • To generate compliance, portfolio, and reporting outputs at the direction of the RIA.
  • To meet legal, regulatory, and security obligations.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use client account data for advertising.

4. How we protect information

We apply industry-standard safeguards including encryption in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256), access controls, audit logging, and US-based data residency. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, but we maintain administrative, technical, and physical measures designed to protect the information we hold.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We share information only with service providers who support our operations under contractual confidentiality obligations, with the RIA whose data we process, or when required by law, regulation, or valid legal process. We do not share client account data with third parties for their own purposes.

6. Data retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the services, comply with our legal and regulatory obligations (including records that RIAs are required to keep under SEC and state rules), resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

7. Your choices and rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to legal and regulatory retention requirements. Contact us at ai@bracciacap.com to make a request.

8. Children's privacy

Our platform is intended for business use by financial professionals and is not directed to individuals under 18.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
